## Key Compliance Requirements

### Data Breach Notification

```
Timeline: 72 hours to notify BSSN/Kominfo
Required: For breaches affecting personal data
Penalty: Administrative fines up to 2% of annual revenue
```

### Electronic System Registration

```
Requirement: Register with Kominfo
Applies to: All public electronic systems
Deadline: Per regulation timeline
```

### Critical Infrastructure Protection

```
Requirement: Implement security controls per BSSN guidelines
Applies to: Designated critical infrastructure
Reporting: Regular security assessments to BSSN
```

## Penalties Overview

| Violation | Law | Maximum Penalty |
|-----------|-----|-----------------|
| Unauthorized Access | UU ITE Art. 30 | 8 years imprisonment |
| Data Interference | UU ITE Art. 32 | 10 years imprisonment |
| System Interference | UU ITE Art. 33 | 10 years imprisonment |
| Illegal Content Distribution | UU ITE Art. 27 | 6 years imprisonment |
| False Information | UU ITE Art. 28 | 6 years imprisonment |
| Data Breach Non-Disclosure | UU PDP | 2% annual revenue |
| Non-Compliance | UU PDP | Administrative fines |
