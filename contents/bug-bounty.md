## Bug Bounty Tips for Indonesian Hunters

### Getting Started

1. **Start with local programs** - Gojek, Tokopedia, and Shopee have active programs.
2. **Learn the stack** - Most Indonesian apps use Go, Java, or Node.js.
3. **Understand the ecosystem** - Indonesian apps often integrate with e-wallets and banks.
4. **Master API testing** - Many Indonesian apps are API-first.
5. **Study OWASP Top 10** - Foundation for web security testing.

### Common Vulnerability Patterns in Indonesian Apps

| Vulnerability | Prevalence | Notes |
|---------------|------------|-------|
| IDOR | High | Often in payment/balance endpoints |
| Business Logic | High | Complex payment flows |
| SSRF | Medium | Internal API exposure |
| Race Conditions | Medium | Payment/balance manipulation |
| Mass Assignment | Medium | Profile/settings endpoints |
| JWT Issues | Medium | Token handling in mobile apps |

## Legal Considerations

> **Important:** Indonesian law (UU ITE) regulates cyber activities. Always:
> - Get written authorization before testing.
> - Stay within scope of programs.
> - Report vulnerabilities responsibly.
> - Do not access or modify user data.
> - Do not cause service disruption.

See [Laws & Regulations](laws.md) for detailed legal reference.
